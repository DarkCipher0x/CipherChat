What protects the chat

  • End-to-end encryption on your own device: the server stores only ciphertext it can't read.
  • Post-quantum hybrid cryptography: ML-KEM-1024 + X25519 key exchange, ML-DSA-87 + Ed25519 signatures, AES-256-GCM.
  • Your keys sealed with a passphrase only you know; identities verified person to person with safety numbers.
  • Encrypted pictures, videos and files; burn-after-reading messages; rooms re-keyed when people leave.
  • Sign-in with password and authenticator code; post-quantum TLS on the connection.

The full details, including what the server can still see and what no encryption can prevent, are in the privacy & security statement.
