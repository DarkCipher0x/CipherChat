Sign in to the secure chat

End-to-end encrypted with post-quantum cryptography. This sign-in opens the chat only: it never gives access to anything else.

A passkey (saved in Bitwarden, Windows Hello, your phone or a security key) signs you in with one tap. Add one in your account after signing in. Then you'll unlock your chat keys with your separate chat passphrase. Invited? Use the link in your invitation email to set your password and authenticator first.

Set up your account

Checking your link…

  1. Open your authenticator (Bitwarden, Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password…) and add an account. Bitwarden: in this site's login item, use the authenticator key (TOTP) field to scan the QR code or paste the key (Bitwarden Premium), or add it to the free Bitwarden Authenticator app. Bitwarden then fills the code for you at sign-in.
  2. Scan this QR code, or choose Enter a setup key (time-based) and paste the key:
    On a phone you can instead open it in your authenticator app.
  3. Enter the 6-digit code the app now shows.

All set. Sign in with your username, password and authenticator code, then choose your chat passphrase.
